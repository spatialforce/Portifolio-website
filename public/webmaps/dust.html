Saharan dust

 

Dust optical depth is how much the dust dims sunlight passing straight down through the air. At 1 the dust blocks about 63% of sunlight coming straight down. Streaks follow the direction the dust is carried and are sped up so you can see them move. Click anywhere on the map for its dust through the event.
